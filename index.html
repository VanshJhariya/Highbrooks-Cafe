A table for whoever you came as.

Some days you need a quiet corner and a laptop. Some days it's six friends, one big table and a plate of waffles that disappears in minutes. The Highbrooks makes room for both.

The coffee is great, the desserts are better, and the room is cozy enough to linger in and polished enough to celebrate in. No wrong reason to walk in.
